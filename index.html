How to Play Theo's Generate

🖥️ Desktop Controls

  • W A S D - Move / Strafe
  • Space - Jump / Ascend / Swim Up
  • Shift - Sneak / Descend / Dive Down
  • Space + Space or F - Toggle Flying
  • Left Click - Mine / Dig Block
  • Right Click - Place Block
  • 1 - 9 - Select Hotbar Block
  • G - Open AI Builder
  • E - 80+ Material Catalog
  • Esc - Release Mouse

📱 World Mechanics

  • 🏊 Swimming: Enter water to swim! Space swims up, Shift dives, looking pitches swim direction.
  • 🕊️ Free Flight: Double tap Space or press F to glide effortlessly anywhere in the world!
  • 👕 Dual Hands & Skins: Both hands are visible; customize skins in the Skins menu.
  • ✨ AI Generation: Gemini translates any prompt into a 3D structure!
